One of the key lessons from 2025 that I now have battle scars to prove is: "It's All Physics".

To reach whatever goals you've set for yourself, you need for it to do it's magic.

Now, the magic that I speak of isn't fate or destiny but the sheer amount of momentum that's created in the world when you just _do the work!_

Ayushmaan, my ex-boss and mentor, calls this the science of building a business.

I'd like to genralise this as The Way of Action.

1/ The Invisible 1000 Users

When you do the work, mindlessly, without care of the results (for some time) you make something exist that shouldn't have existed.

Why? 

Because others would try the same thing and reach the same goals as yours.

But the dogma of the world is that almost everything has at least a 1000 users.

And in business, if you know who you're targetting, even 1000 users are enough.

So, against all the rationale, you need to keep at the journey that you're on and wait for it to reach those 1000 users.

2/ Everything Compounds

True value in life comes from compounding.

Nothing of value has ever come from the y=mx curve. It's all falls to the log(x)-ers of the world.

The people who make compounding happen.

3/ Momentum v/s Quantised Efforts

A lot of people discount the effects of momentum.

When you're on a roll -- and how critical it is to maintain that roll.

I'd go as far to say that a resonated quantised effort is almost meaningless than a continuous roll.

Momentum is CRUCIAL for compounding to take place. CRITICAL!!!!

4/ The Difference Between the Mind and the Intellect

 Well, if you're a Hindu or lived in India much you'd know what I'm talking about.

 This is the prefis of the longest poem of the world the Bhagawat Gita.

 Your mind like to run on an auto-pilot.

 It needs to take shortcuts because taking decisions actually costs energy. Energy that it's saving for moments where you need to decide weather to leave a convo or fix-up some folks.

 Intellect however is the secondary mind.

 It knows all the answers. It's absorbed all the great works.

 It doesn't why for either supremacy or control. 

 But, as the decision maker for yourself, you have to decide who to give control to.

 The mind or the intellect.

 And the days that intellect takes over -- those results compound too.
